# Positive Support

Need help with Positive? Email positiveappsupport@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

## Getting good results

- **Light from behind.** Place the film on a lightbox, a tablet or a phone showing plain white.
- **Turn off other lights.** Room light reflects off the film and adds glare.
- **Shoot straight on.** Hold your iPhone parallel to the film, keep the film flat and fill the frame.
- **Include some film border.** Positive uses the clear film between frames to work out the right colours.

## Common questions

**The colours look off.**
In the editor, open the **Film** tab, tap **Film Base**, then tap the clear film border between frames. Or tap **Auto** to measure again.

**Can I match an original print?**
Yes. In the Film tab, tap **Match Print** and choose a straight-on photo of the print taken in even light.

**How do I edit a whole roll the same way?**
Touch and hold an edited photo in your library and choose **Copy Edits**. Then select the other photos and tap **Paste Edits**.

**My photo looks inverted.**
Check the film type in the Film tab. Use **Slide** for slide or positive film, and **Color** or **B&W** for negatives.

**Where are my exported photos?**
In the Photos app, in the **Converted Negatives** album if that setting is on in Positive's Settings.

**Does Positive upload my photos?**
No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
