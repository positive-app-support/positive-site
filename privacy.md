# Positive Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 1 November 2026*

Positive is made by Harry Orford. This policy explains how the Positive app for iPhone handles your information.

## The short version

Positive does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

## What the app accesses

- **Camera.** Used only while you're photographing negatives in Positive's camera. Images are kept on your device.
- **Photos.** Used to import photos you choose and to save your converted photos. If you choose to save a conversion as an edit of the original, the edit is stored in your photo library by iOS.
- **Files.** Used to import images you choose from the Files app.

You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in Settings on your iPhone.

## What we collect

Nothing. Positive has:

- no accounts or sign-in
- no analytics or tracking
- no advertising
- no third-party SDKs
- no network connection: the app never sends your photos or any other data off your device

Your imported negatives, conversions and edit settings are stored only inside the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Photos you've saved to your photo library stay there.

## Children

Positive doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
